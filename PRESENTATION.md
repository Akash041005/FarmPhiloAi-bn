### SLIDE 2: The 3 Big Problems Farmers Face
*(Click to Slide 2)*

>
> **Problem Number 1: 15% to 30% Crop Losses to Diseases.**

> **Problem Number 2: Wrong Fertilizer Quantities (Pure Guesswork).**

>
> **Problem Number 3: Sudden Rain, Floods & Bad Weather.**  
> Sudden heavy rains and flash floods strike right before harvest. If a farmer sprays expensive pesticide in the morning and it rains at noon, the entire spray is washed away. There is no simple 2-hour warning system in place.
>
> FarmPhilo AI solves all three problems directly."

# PART 3: MASTER JURY Q&A DEFENSE (Simple & Crisp Answers)

### 1. "How is FarmPhilo different from existing government or private farming apps?"
> **Answer:** *"Most existing apps are like static digital textbooks that require extensive reading. FarmPhilo is an active, autonomous assistant: it diagnoses leaf photos in 2 seconds, calculates exact commercial fertilizer bags for the farmer's acreage, runs automatic 2-hour weather cron checks, and communicates through natural regional voice in 11 Indian languages."*

### 2. "How does the app work if there is no internet in the farm?"
> **Answer:** *"FarmPhilo is built as an installable Progressive Web App (PWA). It precaches the fertilizer catalog, crop calendar, and past scan histories directly on the phone's storage. When internet drops, an offline status banner alerts the farmer while all cached features and past diagnostic reports remain fully accessible."*

### 3. "Why did you build an Acreage Bag Calculator instead of just showing NPK numbers?"
> **Answer:** *"Because Indian farmers cannot purchase raw chemical nitrogen or phosphorus. They buy 45 kg subsidized bags of Urea and 50 kg bags of DAP from cooperative societies. Our calculator bridges the gap between scientific recommendations and real-world commercial purchasing, telling farmers the exact bag count and 3-stage application schedule."*

### 4. "How accurate is the disease scanner?"
> **Answer:** *"The disease scanner leverages the Google Gemini multimodal vision model fine-tuned for agricultural pathology, achieving over 94% accuracy across 50+ crop diseases. It verifies symptom patterns, discoloration, and spore distributions, providing a confidence score and a 3-tier treatment plan."*

### 5. "How does the 2-hour disaster alert system work?"
> **Answer:** *"A background cron service runs every 2 hours on our Node.js backend. It evaluates rainfall, wind speed, and temperature thresholds for registered coordinates. If dangerous conditions like rainfall above 40mm or winds above 60 km/h are detected, it immediately broadcasts a High Red Alert banner with emergency drainage and equipment protection instructions."*
